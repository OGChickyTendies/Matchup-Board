Defense vs. run and pass

Starters: Ourlads depth charts, updated Oct 5–7, 2026 (first-string QB, RB, three WR slots, TE), with backups swapped in for starters ruled out. Injury tags for skill players, offensive lines and defenses: the official NFL injury report (practice participation and game status), with CBS Sports as a backup (Q questionable, D doubtful, O out); final statuses come Friday and inactives 90 minutes before kickoff. Defenders on injured reserve: Ourlads reserve lists, with games started from Pro Football Reference. Weather: RotoWire forecasts. Lines: CBS Sports odds (spreads and totals move during the week). Defense: NFL.com team defense stats, 2026 regular season. Fantasy points: nflverse player stats, added after each game ends (usually the next morning). Standard scoring is 1 point per 25 passing yards, 4 per passing TD, −2 per interception, 1 per 10 rushing or receiving yards, 6 per rushing or receiving TD, −2 per fumble lost and 2 per two-point conversion; PPR adds 1 per catch and half PPR 0.5. Weeks 1–4 lineups are who actually played: the QB with the most pass attempts, the RB with the most carries, and the three WRs and the TE with the most targets. Schedule: CBS Sports. Times are Mountain.
